**Current development baseline: 24 October 2026.** This wiki includes development after the published Early Access releases. Older downloads and saved games can have earlier rules; numbers remain subject to balance changes.

Read the [short gameplay and artwork overview](../NEW_HORIZONS_CHANGELOG.md) for the broad comparison. [Downloads and installation instructions](https://github.com/thegandalf196/new-horizons/releases) are supplied with the releases. Your own Heroes III Complete installation is required; original game assets are not included.

## Coverage

The Skill/perk and ordinary combat spell rosters are covered in full. Starting Skills and perks are listed for all 144 authored hero profiles, alongside all 18 classes' base attributes and growth. Individual specialty descriptions still cover selected heroes. All 126 ordinary town recruits have statistics and recruitment entries; individual ability notes currently focus on selected changed lines. Town coverage explains Guilds and adventure magic, rather than every building and prerequisite.

Still to add: hero armies, spellbooks/equipment, biographies and the remaining individual specialties; further creature ability descriptions and dwelling construction costs; town-building tables; artifacts; and dedicated siege/war-machine and adventure-control guides. Future systems such as Governors and Caravans are distinguished below.

## Artwork and future systems

Academy's desert setting, Cabirs, turbaned Magi and Wisps accompany the gameplay changes. The interface adds Order controls, six-school spellbook presentation, perk choices and specialty icons. Selected new artwork remains provisional; the [short changelog](../NEW_HORIZONS_CHANGELOG.md#artwork-and-presentation) summarizes the visual changes.

Governors, regional administration and Caravans are future systems. They are not active mechanics described by this guide.
