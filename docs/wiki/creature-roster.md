# Town creature roster

[Wiki home](README.md) · [Creature abilities and changes](creatures.md) · [Combat](combat.md) · [Leadership](progression.md#leadership-and-stack-capacity) · [Towns](towns.md)

The **126 creatures in the nine ordinary town rosters** form 63 base/upgrade lines: **52 Core, 56 Elite and 18 Champion** creatures. Each town has seven recruitment lines. Neutral, campaign-only and other special creatures outside those lines are not listed here.

These are current ordinary base values, before terrain, hero, spell, Order, artifact or stack-experience modifiers. This is a statistics and recruitment reference; [Creature abilities and changes](creatures.md) explains selected abilities, including Cabir Repair, Genie wishes and Freezing Touch. Numbers can differ in older downloads or campaigns.

## Reading the tables

- **A / D:** intrinsic creature Attack / Defense. Hero Attack and Defense are not added directly to these values.
- **Speed** determines movement; **Initiative** determines activation order. **†** marks a legacy Speed-based Initiative default. For those creatures, Speed changes can also affect Initiative; unmarked entries have a separately defined base Initiative.
- **Shots:** ordinary ammunition. Zero means no ordinary ranged shots; creature spells and activated abilities have their own rules.
- **Gold and other resources:** the recruitment price **per creature**, before discounts. A dash means no additional resource is required.
- **Growth/week:** the base recruitment pool before Citadel/Castle, horde buildings, Grail, external-dwelling, week/event and hero modifiers. Base and upgraded forms share **one pool**: repeating growth on both rows does not grant it twice. Academy's relevant growth building adds to Mage growth separately from this base.
- **Leadership:** requirement per creature, applied independently in every army slot. Each ordinary upgrade retains its base form's requirement.
- **↳** marks the upgraded form of the preceding creature. Town rows follow the actual recruitment-line order, not a hidden numerical-tier ranking.

## Recruitment-line notes

- Academy is the former Tower faction. Its fourth line recruits Genies; its fifth recruits Magi. Cabirs occupy the former Gremlin line. The ordinary Golem line is **Stone Golem → Iron Golem**.
- Conflux's sixth line recruits **Wisp → Greater Wisp**, rather than Psychic/Magic Elementals. Wisps are Core despite that dwelling position; all four classical Elemental families are Elite.
- Storm Elementals have 16 shots; Ice Elementals have zero and fight in melee. Their special control mechanic is explained under [Frozen and Shatter](combat.md#frozen-and-shatter).

These tables do not describe every creature ability or the resources needed to construct its dwelling. The [town guide](towns.md) and [ability overview](creatures.md) cover their respective topics.
