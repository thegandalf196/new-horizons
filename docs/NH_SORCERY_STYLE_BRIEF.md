# Sorcery visual brief

The general direction is in [NH_ART_DIRECTION.md](NH_ART_DIRECTION.md); native family geometry is in [asset specifications](NH_ART_ASSET_SPECIFICATIONS.md) and [school consumer contract](NH_SCHOOL_ART_COMPLETENESS.md).

Sorcery must read as arcane manipulation rather than an elemental lightning/fire attack or necromantic emblem. A meaningful physical subject or coherent group should dominate at 44/32px. Preserve modeled materials, dramatic light/shadow, selective jewel highlights and vintage fantasy illustration, without literal product photography, flat pictograms, dense neon or glossy mobile-game chrome.

Historical armillary, book/key and hand studies are not new commissions or automatically approved motifs. Retain their masters/rejected alternatives privately. Do not repeat background-color generation: the Skill illustration and native leather container are separate. Spell symbols instead retain transparent space around and through their silhouette.

One actual native-size proof precedes family expansion. Record generation/edit provenance, alpha, placement, rank/state distinctions and user approval separately from dimensions or a successful export. Tool availability must be reported honestly; no specific plugin or external account is required by this brief.
