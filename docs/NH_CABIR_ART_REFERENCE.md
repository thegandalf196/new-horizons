# Cabir artwork reference

This is visual direction, not gameplay authority. Gameplay is specified in
the [private game-design canon](https://github.com/thegandalf196/new-horizons-docs/blob/main/canon/game-design.md).

## Selected identity and motion

Adapt the original Fortress shooting Lizardman: slightly shorter, bow removed,
body recolored red. Use its native animation as the motion baseline rather than
independently generated poses that drift in proportions. Both Cabir forms are
ranged; preserve existing fire/Water defenses and Master Repair behavior.
Both forms spit fire from the mouth with empty hands. Preserve the selected
`NH_CabirHandoffFireball.def` projectile binding and align its origin to the
mouth-emission shooting animation without changing damage or timing rules.
This direction does not establish rendered projectile alignment.

The older lava/trousers generated design and its proportion studies are not the
selected replacement identity. Standing previews are not complete walk cycles.
Preserve shared geometry, camera, width, height, tail and whole-sole contact across
contact/passing/recovery frames. Different frame hashes do not prove good motion.

## Packaging and acceptance

Purchaser archives remain read-only. Selected edited Cabir runtime resources belong
in NewHorizons.nhart with provenance; an earlier blanket private-only instruction
for derivative pixels is superseded by the current selected-art packaging policy.
Unchanged original archives remain external, and packaging authorization does not
waive rights or attribution obligations.

Keep originals, superseded studies, prompts and recovery materials locally.
Use actual art-generation/editing capabilities and the maintained art guides,
not a mandatory external plugin/workspace. Selection and provisional in-game
delivery do not imply Final approval or full-animation acceptance. Inspect native
standing, movement, shooting and projectile alignment before declaring the
replacement complete. Do not replace a validated playable cycle on static evidence.
