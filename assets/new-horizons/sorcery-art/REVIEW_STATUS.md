# Sorcery procedural studies: bounded verification limits

Historical concepts remain unaccepted and are not approved runtime resources.
The reported reproducibility check compared86 files from the tested exporter:
40SVG,40PNG,4sheets, exporter and manifest. Its byte equality is scoped to those
preserved inputs/settings; it does not establish visual quality or later results.

The stricter audit reported16 failed PNGs: both families'3 small Skills,
emblem and4buttons touch a canvas edge with partial alpha. Small Skill edge
maxima included alpha1 at top and51 at bottom; medallion bottom reached119.
Dimension/transparency checks alone had missed this condition. Reject wrong
dimensions/modes, blank/opaque output, dirty header margin and clipped exterior.
Correct source geometry/inset rather than clearing clipped pixels after rendering.

Recorded bookmark mean-luminance differences do not establish distinguishable
selection in composed UI. Rank progression and selected/unselected contrast
require actual native/consumer review. Preserve old sources unchanged; a future
revision needs its own reproducibility/audit evidence, visual selection and
rendered acceptance. No submitted concept is selected by this technical record.
