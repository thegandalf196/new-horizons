# Sorcery study production-method constraints

Historical procedural concepts are unaccepted; a proposed rendering method or
composition does not select a motif or approve a family. Material volume,
light-defined edges and native44/32 readability need actual artwork review;
additional flat shapes, uniform noise or gradients do not establish that quality.

A reproducible original render requires editable source geometry, deliberate
camera/lights/materials, fixed seed/settings, tool version and source/export
hashes. Repeatability binds the tested renderer/device/settings, not every GPU
or future software version. Software installation alone is not art acceptance.
Keep purchaser references out of scene textures, editable sources and uploads.

Painted or externally generated alternatives need honest actual-tool provenance,
source identities and applicable rights. Neither external art nor its inputs
automatically inherit a procedural software/art dedication. Preserve rejected
sources privately. Master/native visual review, role/rank/state completeness,
technical reproducibility, rights, selected integration and in-game acceptance
are separate gates. This historical method review authorizes no new production
batch, software installation, account, upload or runtime import.
