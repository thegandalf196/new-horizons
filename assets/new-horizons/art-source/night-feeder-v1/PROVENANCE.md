# Night Feeder Advanced perk icon — provisional

Original artwork created with the host's built-in image generator and the
`homm3-art` workflow. No original Heroes III artwork, purchaser-supplied game
assets, or image reference was used. The generated 1254×1254 master is retained
in `master.png`; the export manifest records its hash and the deterministic
LANCZOS reductions. Four runtime button states were derived from the 44×44
normal raster using the existing active-perk brightness/saturation convention.

Prompt (no image reference):

> Use case: stylized-concept
> Asset type: original square Advanced perk icon for a Heroes III-inspired fantasy strategy game, high-resolution master for reduction to 44×44
> Primary request: Night Feeder, a perk that strengthens the healing return from Vampirism. Create a distinct physical icon, separate from a chalice spell icon: one blackened-silver crescent feeding medallion with two inward-curving polished steel fangs fixed into the metal, drawing a single deep crimson life-thread toward a small central blood-red cabochon. The crescent suggests night; the fangs and drawn-in life-thread suggest feeding. It is an object, not a creature portrait.
> Scene/backdrop: subdued dark umber and oxblood leather with a little dusky blue-violet in the shadows, low-value and quiet.
> Subject: one substantial antique black iron crescent medallion with a clear silhouette, two short inward-curving silver fangs, and one deep crimson thread curling between the fangs into the central red stone. Keep the arrangement tightly integrated and readable.
> Style/medium: late-1990s Western PC fantasy illustration, miniature modeled fantasy still-life painting with pre-rendered 3D volume and painterly fantasy-book character; rich metal and stone materials without photographic realism.
> Composition/framing: close three-quarter view, centered, dense and dominant with narrow safe inset; the medallion fills most of the square without clipping.
> Lighting/mood: theatrical chiaroscuro, small brilliant steel and ruby highlights, deep shadows, restrained magical intensity.
> Color palette: blackened silver, steel-grey, oxblood, umber, one saturated deep-crimson accent; no neon.
> Materials/textures: worn reflective metal, polished fangs, smooth ruby cabochon, softly grained leather background; soft modeled contours, no graphic outline.
> Constraints: high-resolution square master; strong large value masses and one unmistakable silhouette that survives reduction to 44×44. No chalice, face, head, skull, bat, animal, person, hands, gore, splashes, extra props, text, letters, runes, border, interface chrome, watermark, flat vector art, cartoon outlines, intentional pixel art, dithering, plastic gloss, or modern glossy mobile-game treatment.

The 44×44 and 32×32 reductions are retained for inspection. Runtime files are
`NH_perk_night_feeder_normal.png`, `_pressed.png`, `_disabled.png`, and
`_highlighted.png`; they remain 44×44 RGBA. Normal uses the LANCZOS-reduced
painting. Pressed is brightness ×0.82; disabled is saturation ×0.15 then
brightness ×0.65; highlighted is brightness ×1.13. These are presentation
states, not separate generated art.

Visual inspection at native 44×44 shows the crescent, fangs, ruby, and crimson
thread remain recognizable as a distinct perk mark. Artwork status:
**Provisional**, pending in-game visual review and user approval.
