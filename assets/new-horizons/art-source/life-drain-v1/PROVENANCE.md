# Life Drain spell icon — provisional

Original New Horizons concept art generated with the host's built-in image
generator and the `homm3-art` workflow. No purchaser-supplied or extracted
Heroes III image was used. The local generator path is intentionally omitted
from the export manifest; `master.png` preserves the selected master bytes.

Prompt (no image reference):

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original Heroes III-inspired Shadow
> spell icon, square high-resolution master meant to reduce to 44x44, 32x32,
> and 30x30. Primary request: Life Drain—stolen vitality flows from an enemy
> to an ally. One tightly integrated emblematic still life: a dark, withered
> iron-gauntleted hand on the left yielding a single thick crimson stream of
> life energy into an open, healthy pale-gold armored hand on the right. The
> red stream is the unmistakable focal shape and connects both hands; avoid
> separate floating symbols. Very close-up, fills most of square with a narrow
> safe inset; readable at tiny sizes. Late-1990s Western PC high-fantasy
> miniature modeled painting, rich aged metal/leather material, theatrical
> chiaroscuro, warm highlight on receiving hand and deep shadow on draining
> hand, dark umber and oxblood textured background. Saturated crimson with
> restrained glow, soft modeled contours, pre-rendered fantasy-book character.
> No person, face, text, letters, runes, icon border, interface chrome, modern
> flat/vector art, cartoon outlines, neon particle cloud, watermark, or
> intentional pixel art.

Exported from the 1254×1254 master using the skill's LANCZOS helper. The
44×44, 32×32, and 30×30 icons preserve the dark-hand → crimson stream →
gold-hand silhouette. Art is **Provisional** until actual in-game visual review
and user approval; no final-art claim is made.
