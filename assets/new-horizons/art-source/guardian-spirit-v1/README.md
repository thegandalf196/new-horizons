# Guardian Spirit spell icon — v1

Status: **Provisional original concept art**. Candidate icon exports exist, but
the spell's runtime graphics binding, in-game visual review, and final-art
approval are not established.

The image depicts a luminous guardian turning aside a dark iron bolt. It is
intended to suggest a temporary protective spirit and physical-hit interception,
while remaining visually distinct from the conventional shield in Holy Armor
and the sanctuary architecture in Sanctuary. It was generated as original art;
no game artwork, reference image, or user-supplied image was used as input.

- Copy hashes: [`runtime-manifest.json`](runtime-manifest.json).

Native-size inspection found the guardian's pale face/wing silhouette readable
at 44, 32, and 30 pixels. The deflected dark bolt remains a supporting cue but
its finer impact detail softens at 30 pixels. The helper comparison presents
44×44 and 32×32 at 1:1; the 30×30 export was inspected separately at 1:1.
These raster checks do not establish spell-slot correctness, in-game rendering,
or user approval.
