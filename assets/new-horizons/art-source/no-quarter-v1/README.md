# No Quarter provisional art v1

Original purpose-made artwork for `new-horizons:offense.noQuarter`, generated
with the built-in image generator through the `homm3-art` workflow on
2026-09-27. The first pass was edited once to bring the gauntlet and full shield
rim inside the frame. No user reference image, purchaser art, existing game art,
or external style-reference pixels were used. The exact prompts and opaque host
output IDs are recorded in `prompt-no-quarter*.txt` and `generation.json`.

The selected 1254×1254 master, deterministic 44×44 and 32×32 LANCZOS reductions,
labeled comparison sheet, and source hash manifest are under `exports/no-quarter/`.
`export-runtime.py` derives four 44×44 VCMI button states with brightness and
color adjustments only. The runtime copies and descriptor are retained under
`runtime/` and installed under `Mods/new-horizons/Images/`; their hashes are in
`runtime-manifest.json`. The client binding uses `NH_perk_no_quarter`.

Status: Provisional; no final art approval or in-game visual acceptance is
recorded. The image depicts a steel gauntlet breaking a sword down across a
cracked iron-rimmed shield. At 44 pixels the gauntlet, round shield, and
downward broken blade remain the intended large visual cues; small cracks and
metal wear merge into texture.
