# Countercharge provisional art v1

Original purpose-made artwork for `new-horizons:armorer.countercharge`, generated
with the built-in image generator through the `homm3-art` workflow on
2026-09-27. No user image or external style-reference pixels were used. The
exact generation prompt and host output identifier are retained in
`prompt-countercharge.txt` and `generation.json`.

The selected 1254×1254 master, deterministic 44×44 and 32×32 LANCZOS
reductions, labeled comparison sheet, and source hash manifest are under
`exports/countercharge/`. `export-runtime.py` derives four 44×44 VCMI button
states with brightness and color adjustments only. The runtime copies and
descriptor are retained under `runtime/` and installed under
`Mods/new-horizons/Images/`; their hashes are in `runtime-manifest.json`. The
client binding uses `NH_perk_countercharge`.

Status: Provisional; no final art approval or in-game visual acceptance is
recorded. The painting shows a battered kite shield braced into earth, an
incoming sword rebounding from its right rim, and a counterblade projecting
from behind the shield to the left. At native 44×44 and 32×32, the shield and
opposed blade directions remain distinguishable; fine rivets, scratches, and
ground texture merge into the materials as intended.
