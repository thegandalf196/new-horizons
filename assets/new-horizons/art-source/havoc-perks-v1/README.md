# Havoc perk provisional art v1

Original provisional paintings generated with the built-in image generator on
2026-09-21, then reduced from the retained square masters with the HoMM3 art
export helper. Runtime exports are 44x44 PNGs with mechanically derived pressed,
disabled, and highlighted states.

- `NH_perk_conductor_master.png`: copper control rod linking three iron rings
  with blue-white lightning, expressing controlled Chain Lightning jumps.
- `NH_perk_annihilator_master.png`: blackened warhammer breaking a silver
  breastplate along a red magical fissure, expressing protection bypass.

Both prompts requested original late-1990s modeled fantasy still-life paintings,
strong silhouettes readable at 44x44, and no text, borders, UI chrome, deliberate
pixel art, or watermark. These assets are provisional and may be replaced by
later user-approved commissioned art.
