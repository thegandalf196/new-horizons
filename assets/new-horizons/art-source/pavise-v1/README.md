# Pavise provisional art v1

Purpose-made original artwork for `new-horizons:armorer.pavise`, generated
with the built-in image generator through the `homm3-art` workflow on
2026-09-27. No user image or external style-reference pixels were used. The
exact generation prompt and host output identifier are retained in
`prompt-pavise.txt` and `generation.json`.

The selected 1254×1254 master, deterministic 44×44 and 32×32 LANCZOS
reductions, labeled comparison sheet, and source hash manifest are under
`exports/pavise/`. `export-runtime.py` derives four 44×44 VCMI button states
with brightness and color adjustments only. Runtime copies and the descriptor
are retained under `runtime/` and installed under
`Mods/new-horizons/Images/`; their hashes are in `runtime-manifest.json`. The
client binding uses `NH_perk_pavise`.

Status: Provisional; no final art approval or in-game visual acceptance is
recorded. The master shows a grounded wooden pavise with two embedded arrows
protecting a braced armored defender. At native 44×44 and 32×32, the grounded
shield silhouette, incoming arrows, and defender behind it remain
distinguishable; plank grain and small fittings merge into the wood and iron
as intended.
