# Active perk provisional art v5

This set begins closing the remaining active-perk art gaps with distinct icons
created through the `homm3-art` workflow.

- Arcane Acquisition: a brass astrolabe and crystal prism split arcane light
  toward two newly marked targets.
- Spell Buffer: a brass-caged crystal reservoir stores bright surplus magic
  above a quieter reserve.
- Stormcaller: a copper storm horn draws lightning from a spiral of dark cloud.
- Intelligence: an ordered sapphire mind-crystal draws knowledge from an open
  spellbook.
- Illusionist: a silver mirror repeats a violet crystal into unreal copies.
- Spellbinder: a clasped black grimoire gathers five streams of high magic.
- Pathfinding: a travel boot follows safe stones between thorns and marsh.
- Navigation: a brass sextant and compass point toward a lighthouse.
- Scouting: a spyglass reveals a watchtower and illuminated terrain.
- Volunteer Network: village pennants and seals converge on a muster roll.
- Elite Draft: a polished officer's helm is selected from common equipment.
- Champion's Call: a ceremonial war horn opens the champion's gate.
- Master Recruiter: a command seal completes an all-tier muster ledger.
- Swift Gate: a nearly emptied hourglass stands inside a rushing portal.
- Wide Gate: two rings of marked battlefield hexes surround an infernal gate.
- Hellfire Arrival: a newly opened gate blasts fire into adjacent directions.
- Reinforced Gate: iron braces and a crimson force shield protect the portal.
- Mobile Gate: ember footprints lead from an infernal greave into a portal.
- Infernal Beacon: a gate, allied banner, and beacon share an Initiative sign.
- Reserve Discipline: an iron war mask holds a broken morale mark level.
- Endless Legion: ruined reserve arms return as embers through a barred gate.

The high-resolution master is retained alongside 44x44 and 32x32 LANCZOS
reductions and a comparison sheet. `export-runtime.py` derives the four VCMI
button states using brightness and saturation changes only.

This artwork is provisional and has not been designated definitive by the
user.
