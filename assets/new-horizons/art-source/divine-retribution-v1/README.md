# Divine Retribution spell icon — v1

Status: **Provisional original concept art**. It has not received final-art
approval or in-game visual review.

The icon shows an antique-gold justice scale tilted toward a dark attacker’s
blade as a small ivory-gold flame catches on it. The scale communicates
judgment; the concentrated ignition suggests holy retaliation beginning after
the attack. Its silhouette and subject differ from Guardian Spirit’s pale
guardian deflecting a bolt and Holy Armor’s breastplate inside a shield aura.

## Inspection

The justice-scale silhouette remains identifiable at native 44×44, 32×32, and
30×30. The lower dark blade and its small bright holy ignition remain visible
as supporting cues at all three sizes. The 44×44 and 32×32 samples and their
nearest-neighbor enlargements are in the labeled comparison sheet; 30×30 was
inspected separately at native size. The master has a subdued textured
background, modeled worn brass and iron, a strong light/shadow split, and no
frame or text.

This inspection covers raster appearance only. It does not establish spell
data binding, in-game rendering, or gameplay acceptance.
