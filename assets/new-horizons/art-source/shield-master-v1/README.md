# Shield Master provisional art v1

Purpose-made original artwork for `new-horizons:armorer.shieldMaster`, generated
with the built-in image generator through the `homm3-art` workflow on
2026-09-27. No user image or external style-reference pixels were used. The
exact generation prompt and host output identifier are retained in
`prompt-shield-master.txt` and `generation.json`.

The selected 1254×1254 master, deterministic 44×44 and 32×32 LANCZOS
reductions, labeled comparison sheet, and source hash manifest are under
`exports/shield-master/`. `export-runtime.py` derives four 44×44 VCMI button
states with brightness and color adjustments only. Runtime copies and the
descriptor are retained under `runtime/` and installed under
`Mods/new-horizons/Images/`; their hashes are in `runtime-manifest.json`. The
client binding uses `NH_perk_shield_master`.

Status: Provisional; no final art approval or in-game visual acceptance is
recorded. The master shows two incoming weapons simultaneously striking a large
battered shield in front of a smaller polished shield. At native 44×44 and
32×32, the dominant shield, tucked smaller shield, and opposed impact points
remain distinguishable; fine scratches and rivets merge into the steel as
intended.
