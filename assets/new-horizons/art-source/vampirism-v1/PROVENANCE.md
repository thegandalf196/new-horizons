# Vampirism combat spell icon — provisional

Original artwork created with the host's built-in image generator and the
`homm3-art` workflow. No original Heroes III artwork, purchaser-supplied game
assets, or image reference was used. The generated 1254×1254 master is retained
in `master.png`; the export manifest records its hash and the deterministic
LANCZOS derivatives. The runtime rasters are opaque RGB PNGs.

Prompt (no image reference):

> Use case: stylized-concept
> Asset type: original square Heroes III-inspired combat spell icon, high-resolution master for reduction to 44×44, 32×32, and 30×30
> Primary request: Vampirism, a universal lifesteal enchantment. Show one dominant physical object: a dark iron chalice, filling most of the square, with a single deep crimson life-thread curling into its open bowl. It should read as an enchantment that lets any unit draw life, not as a vampire creature or portrait.
> Scene/backdrop: subdued, textured umber and oxblood leather, low-value and quiet behind the object.
> Subject: one heavy aged blackened-iron chalice with a substantial silhouette, raised rim and simple hammered planes; one narrow continuous crimson life-thread curls down into the bowl, with a tiny bright red highlight to separate it from the dark metal. No other focal object.
> Style/medium: late-1990s Western PC fantasy illustration, miniature modeled fantasy still-life painting with pre-rendered 3D volume and painterly fantasy-book character; material realism without photographic realism.
> Composition/framing: close three-quarter view, centered, dense and unmistakable at tiny size, narrow safe inset so no silhouette is clipped.
> Lighting/mood: theatrical chiaroscuro; bright modeled highlights on one side of the iron, deep shadow on the other, restrained magical intensity.
> Color palette: iron grey, umber, oxblood, one saturated deep crimson accent; no neon.
> Materials/textures: worn reflective iron and softly grained leather; soft modeled contours, not graphic outlines.
> Constraints: high-resolution square master; major value masses and the chalice-plus-thread silhouette must remain clear when reduced. No text, lettering, runes, border, interface chrome, hands, faces, creatures, gore, splashes, flames, extra props, watermark, flat vector art, cartoon outlines, intentional pixel art, dithering, plastic gloss, or modern glossy mobile-game treatment.

Exports: `vampirism-44.png`, `vampirism-32.png`, and `vampirism-30.png`
were reduced from the master with LANCZOS and copied byte-for-byte to the
matching `NH_spell_vampirism_*` runtime names. Visual inspection at 44×44,
32×32, and 30×30 shows the chalice and crimson thread remain distinct.
