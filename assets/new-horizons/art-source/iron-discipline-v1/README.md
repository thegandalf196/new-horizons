# Iron Discipline provisional art v1

Purpose-made original artwork for `new-horizons:armorer.ironDiscipline`,
generated with the built-in image generator through the `homm3-art` workflow on
2026-09-27. No user image or external style-reference pixels were used. The
exact generation prompt and host output identifier are retained in
`prompt-iron-discipline.txt` and `generation.json`.

The selected 1254×1254 master, deterministic 44×44 and 32×32 LANCZOS
reductions, labeled comparison sheet, and source hash manifest are under
`exports/iron-discipline/`. `export-runtime.py` derives four 44×44 VCMI button
states with brightness and color adjustments only. Runtime copies and the
descriptor are retained under `runtime/` and installed under
`Mods/new-horizons/Images/`; their hashes are in `runtime-manifest.json`. The
client binding uses `NH_perk_iron_discipline`.

Status: Provisional; no final art approval or in-game visual acceptance is
recorded. The master shows a battered iron tower shield braced into earth while
a compact blue-violet magical impact splits around it. At native 44×44 and
32×32, the upright shield, grounded base, bright impact, and deflected arcs
remain distinct; fine rivets and surface scratches merge as intended.
